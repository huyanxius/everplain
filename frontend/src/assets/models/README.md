# Model brand assets

These marks identify the AI brands discussed in Everplain's planned model access. They are not Everplain's logo or a statement of partnership. The site labels model availability and pricing as forthcoming.

Source: the [SVGL asset library](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl), retrieved 2026-09-30. SVG shapes and original colors are preserved. The accompanying `LICENSE` contains the library's MIT notice; brand trademarks belong to their respective owners.

- `claude.svg`: [claude-ai-icon.svg](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/static/library/claude-ai-icon.svg), Anthropic Claude mark.
- `chatgpt.svg`: [openai.svg](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/static/library/openai.svg), OpenAI mark, displayed with ChatGPT label.
- `gemini.svg`: [gemini.svg](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/static/library/gemini.svg), Google Gemini mark.
