# Everplain scene assets

Two original cinematic mattes created with the built-in imagegen tool for this website. They are stored here as WebP, 1672 × 941; the website never depends on the generation cache. Existing brand mark, cabinet and projector art are reused from `src/assets/`.

`meadow.webp` is the first view. `library.webp` appears as the visitor scrolls. Three.js renders actual instanced foreground grass over the mattes, with wind and pointer-responsive camera motion. The shelves and machine are photographed-style image elements, not freely navigable 3D models. The same images remain readable when WebGL is unavailable.

## Meadow prompt

Create a premium cinematic website background photograph, very wide landscape 16:9. A quietly surreal meadow at blue hour, exactly the look of a high-end arthouse film shot on fine 35mm film. Extremely detailed long, soft, dark sage and olive grass sweeps over sculptural rolling hills, delicate white wildflowers only in the nearest foreground bottom corners. A gentle winding low valley into mist in the center. Soft silver-blue dusk light grazes the right hill and picks out every blade of grass. The upper 48 percent is almost empty very dark desaturated blue-green sky, seamless into distant atmospheric hills, purposely calm dark negative space for a huge white website title. Foreground grassy hill rises diagonally from the lower right; middle-left meadow lower, not symmetrical. The mood is deeply peaceful, wondrous, tactile, futuristic nostalgia, restrained palette of charcoal blue, grey-green, ivory, absolutely no neon or purple. Nature photographed as an exquisite architectural landscape, realistic delicate botanical detail, analog grain very fine and subtle. No buildings, no people, no text, no logos, no moon, no stars, no fantasy glowing dots, no cards, no UI. Full bleed, no border. High dynamic range but dark readable upper half.

## Library prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural architectural landscape. Asset: panoramic full bleed premium website background, 16:9 wide landscape. An astonishing quiet open-air library standing directly in a wild grassy meadow at blue hour. Futuristic nostalgia, realistic cinematic analog medium-format photograph, NOT cartoon, NOT game low-poly. In right two-thirds of frame, a sculptural curved wall of tall dark walnut library shelves and weathered wooden card catalog drawers, filled with many old linen-bound ivory books, arranged as an incomplete circular reading room with no roof. Old brutalist concrete and brushed metal structural uprights, antique wood juxtaposed with simple very precise modern architecture. In the foreground right, a modest reading table with open book and a lovely small vintage analog reel-to-reel tape recorder with two large silver reels, no computer screens. Slender warm amber reading lamps softly illuminate book edges. Grasses and small white wildflowers grow between concrete stepping stones and beneath the shelves. Background dark desaturated blue-green hills and soft mist under a vast charcoal-blue dusk sky. The architecture is on the right occupying from x=42% to x=95%, y=28% to bottom. The LEFT THIRD must remain very dark empty meadow and sky, smooth low contrast negative space for white title overlay. View from about ten meters away, human eye level, slightly wide lens; the library is architecturally impressive yet inviting, close enough to see book bindings, woodgrain, machined aluminum. Moonless soft cool skylight, warm library light, no neon. Deep dark cinematic shadows with exquisite tactile details, restrained forest green, walnut, silver, aged paper. No text, no visible signage, no people, no logos, no UI, no borders, no floating objects, no fantasy glowing points.
